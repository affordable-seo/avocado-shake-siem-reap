What's in an avocado shake?

In Cambodia, street vendors and small restaurants make avocado shakes by blending avocado flesh with crushed ice and condensed milk. Sugar syrup can be added for extra sweetness.

How do you recognize a good avocado shake?

The ideal avocado shake should be rich and creamy with a dense texture. If yours turns out thin or watery, it's probably over-diluted and needs more avocado flesh. Getting this right can be tricky because ripeness matters—the avocado must be fully ripe, or the shake will have an unpleasant bitter taste.

Language Guide

How to order your avocado shake in Khmer 🇰🇭

If you want to impress the seller, you can order your avocado in Khmer. Here's a quick roundup below:

សូមប័រក្រឡុកមួយ

Phonetic: Som beu krolok muy.

English: "One avocado shake, please"

Avocado (ប័រ - bəə) Pronounce like the "ur" in "burger" — short and soft.
To Blend (ក្រឡុក) kraa like "crane" + lok like "lock"
Som Please — standard way to start a request.
Beu Avocado — derived from the French beurre (butter).
Krolok Shake / Blended
Muy One
Sko tek-tek A little sugar
Sko ha-sep 50% less sugar

Want to take your avocado shake to the next level? Ask the vendor to add an egg yolk — it usually only costs an extra 1,500 riels and makes it incredibly rich.
